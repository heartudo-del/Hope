How this works

  • Every time you open the app, it reads your device's clock, jumps to today's card, and scrolls straight to whatever block you're in right now — no clicking needed.
  • The current block is marked "Now" in dark pink so you can spot it instantly, then you're free to scroll anywhere else in the week. Tap the pink banner at the top to jump back to it.
  • Add this page to your phone's home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or the install prompt on Android) to open it like an app — it keeps working without an internet connection once it's loaded.
